📖 How to Play Ludo

  • 1. Bring Tokens Out: Roll a 6 to move a token from your yard onto the outer track.
  • 2. Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or reaching Home grants an extra roll. Three 6s in a row passes the turn.
  • 3. Captures & Safe Stars: Landing on an opponent token on a regular square sends it home. Tokens on ★ safe stars and colored start squares are safe.
