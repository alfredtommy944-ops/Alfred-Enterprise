📖 How to Play Standard Ludo

  • 1. Bring Tokens Out: Roll a 6 to move a token from your starting yard onto the outer track.
  • 2. Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or reaching Home gives you an extra roll. Three 6s in a row passes your turn.
  • 3. Captures & Safe Stars: Landing on an opponent token on a normal square sends it back to its yard. Tokens on ★ safe squares and colored start squares cannot be captured.
  • 4. Win & Claim Rewards: Guide all 4 of your tokens around the board and into the center Home goal to win a store promo code for Alfred Enterprise (IDA, Kenema).
