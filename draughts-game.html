📖 How to Play Draughts

  • 1. Diagonal Movement: Men move diagonally forward one square onto an empty dark square.
  • 2. Mandatory Captures & Multi-Jumps: When a jump over an opponent piece is available, you must capture. If another jump opens up right after, keep jumping!
  • 3. Kings (♛): Reaching the far back row crowns your piece a King, allowing diagonal moves forward and backward.
  • 4. Winning: Capture all opponent pieces or block all of their legal moves to win the match.
